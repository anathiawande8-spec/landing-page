How it holds together

Structured, not scraped together.

Every paper is built from the same three guarantees, so nothing quietly breaks when you edit one question at 9pm the night before.

01

Editable by question

Papers are generated as structured content, not a block of text — so you can regenerate or rewrite a single question without touching the rest.

02

Reviewed before you see it

A second automated pass checks grammar, mark totals, duplicate questions and formatting before the paper ever reaches your screen.

Tests, exams, worksheets and assignments are generated with a matching memorandum in the same pass — never as an afterthought.
