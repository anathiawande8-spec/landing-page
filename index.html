Questions

Before you start

Is it actually CAPS-aligned?+

Papers are generated against your chosen subject, grade and term with CAPS topics, weighting and cognitive levels in mind. You review and edit every paper before you use it — Pinky drafts, you're always the one who signs off.

Do I need a credit card to try it?+

No. New teachers get one free paper with no card required. Pricing for anything beyond that will be shown clearly before you're ever asked to pay.

Can I edit a paper after it's generated?+

Yes — every question can be edited, regenerated on its own, or removed. The memorandum updates alongside the paper, and marks are checked so they still add up to your target total.

What can I download?+

Both Word and PDF, for the cover page, the question paper and the memorandum — separately or bundled together.
